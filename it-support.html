What we look after

Three areas, one friendly team.

Computers and software

New computer setup, software installation and updates, email and printer setup, and fixes for slow or misbehaving devices.

Wi-Fi and network installation

Home and office networks designed and installed properly, with good coverage in every room and secure settings.

Maintenance and troubleshooting

We find the cause, fix it and explain it. Regular check-ups help problems from coming back.

Who we help

We work with homes and local small businesses only, so our advice always fits your size and budget.

For homes

Working from home, streaming, online learning and family devices. We make the technology just work.

For small businesses

Reliable computers, fast Wi-Fi and a network that supports your team, without hiring an IT department.

How support works

Simple and clear, from first call to fixed.

  1. 1

    Tell us

    Describe the problem by phone, email or our contact form.

  2. 2

    Diagnose

    We investigate remotely or visit you on site.

  3. 3

    Fix

    We solve the problem and test that it’s working.

  4. 4

    Explain

    We show you what we did and how to avoid it next time.

Support questions

Do you work on site or remotely?

Both. We can often fix things remotely, and we visit your home or workplace in Melbourne when needed.

What if I’m not sure what’s wrong?

Just describe what you see. We’ll work out the cause and explain it in plain English.

Can you set up Wi-Fi for my whole home or office?

Yes. We plan the layout, install and configure the equipment, and test coverage in every room.

Do you help small businesses as well as homes?

Yes. We work with households and local small businesses, and tailor our advice to each.

Need technology help?

Tell us what’s going wrong. We’ll reply within one business day. Looking for a website or more customers instead? See our web services.
