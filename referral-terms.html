Referral credit terms

For our November 2026 offer: an AUD 50 credit for existing customers who introduce a new business.

The short version

AUD 50 credit for existing customers who introduce a new business in November 2026. The credit is added after the new business pays in full, and can be used on existing or new services. It has no cash value. Terms apply.

See the November offers

  1. Who can refer: existing SmallNetworks customers only, who have paid for a completed job.
  2. Offer period: November 2026 only. The referred business must pay for its first job between 1 and 30 November 2026.
  3. What counts as a referral: a new business that has not been a SmallNetworks customer before. The referrer introduces them by message, or the new business names the referrer when they get in touch. A referral can’t be claimed after work has started.
  4. When the credit is earned: the AUD 50 credit is added once the referred business has paid for its first job in full between 1 and 30 November 2026.
  5. How the credit can be used: towards SmallNetworks services only, either the referrer’s existing services or new ones. It can’t be exchanged for cash, refunded or transferred.
  6. Limits: one AUD 50 credit per business referred. No self-referrals, and no referring a business owned or run by the referrer themselves.
  7. Expiry: the credit must be used before 30 June 2027.
  8. Changes: SmallNetworks can change or end the offer at any time. Credits already earned will still be honoured.

Questions about these terms? Email contact@smallnetworks.com.au or call 0414 518 017.
