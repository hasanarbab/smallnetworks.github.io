Website questions

Will I own my website?

Yes. Once you’ve paid in full, you own the final design and your content, as set out in our terms. We recommend your domain name is registered in your name, and we’ll tell you if we ever hold anything on your behalf.

Can you redesign my existing website?

Yes. We review what you have, keep what works and rebuild what doesn’t.

Do I need to write all the text?

No. We can write or polish the wording with you. We’ll ask for your logo, photos and key details.

Will it work on phones?

Yes. Every site we build is designed for phones first and tested on tablets and desktops.

What if I need a change after launch?

Send us a message. Care plans cover regular updates, and we’ll quote clearly for any bigger changes.

Ready for a website you’re proud of?

Tell us about your business and what you want your website to do. We’ll reply within one business day.
