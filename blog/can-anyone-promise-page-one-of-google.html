Can anyone promise page one of Google?

An honest look at SEO: why nobody can guarantee a ranking, how long it takes, and what good work looks like.

25 October 2026 · 1 min read · By SmallNetworks

Short answer: no. Google decides who shows up, and nobody outside Google controls that. If someone promises you page one in 30 days, be careful.

Why nobody can promise a ranking

Google looks at a lot of things, and it keeps changing how it works. It also shows different results to different people, depending on where they are and what they searched before. A business can do plenty to help, but it cannot control the result.

How long does SEO take?

It depends on your industry, your area and what your website needs. Some changes can help within weeks. Bigger gains usually take months. A brand new business with a new website should expect it to take longer than a business that has been around for years.

What honest SEO work looks like

  • A complete and accurate Google Business Profile
  • Clear page titles and descriptions on every page
  • A website that loads quickly and works well on phones
  • Pages that say what you do and where you do it
  • Real reviews from real customers

Warning signs

  • A guaranteed ranking, or a guaranteed number of enquiries
  • A secret method they will not explain
  • Reviews that are bought or written by someone else
  • No reports, or reports full of jargon

What we promise

We do careful work, we follow Google’s guidelines and we send plain-English reports so you can see what changed. We do not promise rankings. You can read how it works on our SEO page.
